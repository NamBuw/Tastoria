## 3. Domain Synthesis

### 3.1 P1 — Computer Vision Ingredient Detection & Counting
- **Real-time Detectors & Licensing**: Single-stage YOLO detectors (`Redmon2016yolo` `E014`) evolved through `YOLO11` (`Ultralytics2024yolo11` `E064`) and NMS-free `YOLO26` (`Ultralytics2026yolo26` `E063`, `AGPL-3.0`, ~2.4M params, 38.9 ms CPU ONNX benchmarked by Ultralytics) with `ONNX Runtime` (`ONNXRuntime2025` `E068`) enabling low-latency CPU deployment. Open-vocabulary grounders such as `Grounding DINO` (`Liu2025grounding` `E016`) achieve strong generic zero-shot detection, but `RF100-VL` (`Robicheaux2025rf100vl` `E023`) demonstrates `< 2%` zero-shot performance on hard domain-specific benchmarks—explaining why region-specific Vietnamese raw ingredients (`thit_lon, thit_ga, su_su, muop, rau_cai, rau_muong, gia_do`) require targeted supervised data rather than blind zero-shot auto-labeling.
- **Public Datasets & Preprocessing**: Tastoria's `yolo_v2` integrates `Open Images V7` (`Kuznetsova2020openimages` `E022`, `OpenImages2022v7` `E066`, incl. `IsGroupOf` metadata critical for excluding ambiguous group boxes from counting), `LVIS` (`Gupta2019lvis` `E020`, `1,203` classes), `RPC Checkout` (`Wei2019rpc` `E021`), `Bangladesh Vegetable Dataset` (`Jahan2025bangladesh` `E060`), and `Kitchen Fruit & Vegetable Detection` (`Morros2026kitchen` `E067`). For crowded scenes and small ingredients, `Soft-NMS` (`Bodla2017softnms` `E017`) and `SAHI` slicing (`Akyon2022sahi` `E018`) offer targeted remedies, whereas `Wang2023underwater` (`E019`) demonstrated across 18 enhancement methods × 7 detectors that aggressive image enhancement often degrades detector AP relative to raw images—motivating Tastoria's controlled ablation of `none` vs. `gray-world` vs. `CLAHE` under `Lin2014coco` (`E015`) size-stratified AP.

### 3.2 P2 — Produce Freshness Classification & Probability Calibration
- **Classic Texture/Color vs. Lightweight CNNs**: Reviews by `Brosnan2004inspection` (`E032`) and `Bhargava2021quality` (`E033`) establish color and surface texture descriptors (`Ojala2002lbp` `E029` uniform LBP; `Haralick1973glcm` `E030` GLCM) paired with SVMs (`Platt1999probabilistic` `E028`) as strong interpretable baselines. Specific to leafy greens and local produce, `Koyama2021spinach` (`E034`) modeled 12-day chronological spinach spoilage with sensory scores + SVM, and `Abeer2023freshness` (`E035`) applied transfer learning to chronological vegetable datasets (`AgriFreshNET` `E061`, `Local Spinach Leaf` `E062`). Lightweight CNNs (`Howard2019mobilenetv3` `E024` MobileNetV3, `Tan2019efficientnet` `E025` EfficientNet, `He2016resnet` `E026` ResNet) improve feature representation, though `Afifi2019color` (`E031`) warns that uncompensated white-balance shifts substantially degrade CNN accuracy.
- **Deduplication, Ordinal Agreement & Calibration**: Random image-level splitting after augmentation or across near-identical capture-burst frames induces severe data leakage (e.g., `34.2%` near-duplicate leakage observed in raw `Local Spinach`), requiring D8 dihedral pHash clustering (`2,366` independent groups) and `StratifiedGroupKFold`. Ordinal spoilage evaluation requires `Quadratic Weighted Kappa` (`Cohen1960coefficient` `E011`, `Cohen1968weighted` `E012`) alongside `Macro-F1`. Crucially, `Guo2017calibration` (`E027`) established that modern deep neural networks are systematically overconfident—necessitating out-of-fold **Temperature Scaling** (`Guo2017calibration` `E027`) or **Platt Scaling** (`Platt1999probabilistic` `E028`) evaluated via proper scoring rules (`Brier1950verification` `E013` Brier score, NLL) and Expected Calibration Error (`ECE`) before driving downstream pantry urgency weights and `kiem_tra` abstention logic.

### 3.3 P3 — Meal Recommender Systems & Evaluation Rigor
- **Collaborative, Linear & Hybrid Models**: Classical implicit-feedback recommenders span item-neighborhood cosine similarity (`Sarwar2001itemknn` `E040`), weighted ALS (`Hu2008als` `E038`), pairwise BPR-MF (`Rendle2009bpr` `E036`), closed-form item-item autoencoders `EASE^R` (`Steck2019ease` `E039`), WARP/logistic hybrid factorization `LightFM` (`Kula2015lightfm` `E041`, `Weston2011wsabie` `E058`), and gradient-boosted pairwise/listwise rankers `LambdaMART` (`Burges2010lambdamart` `E059`). Recent replicability research (`Milogradskii2024revisiting` `E037`) demonstrates that BPR performance can vary by up to 50% depending on negative sampling and regularization details—directly explaining Tastoria's observed gap between custom NumPy BPR and `implicit` BPR on `Food.com` (`Majumder2019foodcom` `E042`).
- **Evaluation Leakage & Popularity Dominance**: Four foundational methodological studies govern Tastoria's RecSys protocol: `Dacrema2019progress` (`E044`) showed simple tuned baselines frequently outperform complex neural models; `Krichene2020sampled` (`E047`) proved sampled top-K metrics distort relative model rankings, requiring **full-catalog evaluation**; `Meng2020splitting` (`E045`) and `Ji2023leakage` (`E046`) proved leave-last-one-out leaks future interactions and recommended **global temporal cutoffs** (`d_val=2010-06-24`, `d_test=2012-04-01`). For Vietnamese food data, `Tran2024vifoodrec` (`E057`, `ViFoodRec`) highlights scraping/licensing and median-imputation limits (~40% missing cells filled with medians), justifying Tastoria's curated `180`-dish KB and transparent `SIMULATED=true` synthetic survey pipeline pending real human collection.

### 3.4 P4 — Ingredient Substitution & Constrained Meal Composition
- **Ingredient Substitution**: Co-occurrence embeddings (`Mikolov2013word2vec` `E048`, `Teng2012networks` `E049`) capture both complements and substitutes; `Teng2012networks` explicitly separated recipe modification (substitute) networks from co-occurrence (complement) networks. Subsequent benchmarks—`Food2Vec/FoodBERT` (`Pellegrini2021food2vec` `E050`), `DIISH` (`Shirai2021diish` `E056`, combining embeddings + food knowledge graph category filtering), and `GISMo / Recipe1MSubs` (`Fatemi2023gismo` `E051`)—consistently demonstrate that pure co-occurrence embeddings underperform curated pair tables and hybrid food-group/knowledge-graph fusion. This aligns with Tastoria's baseline observation that filtering `Word2Vec` by raw `PPMI <= 2.0` hurt `MRR` and motivates **Hybrid Fusion** (`Word2Vec + Nutrient Cosine + PMI + Curated`) exported as `100d` normalized vectors for `pgvector`.
- **Constrained Meal Composition & Scheduling**: `Elsweiler2015mealplan` (`E052`), `Trattner2017health` (`E053`), `Toledo2019meal` (`E054`), and `MealRec+` (`Li2024mealrecplus` `E055`) document the fundamental Pareto trade-off between user taste preference and nutritional/health constraints: hard post-filtering severely degrades preference recall (`MealRec+`: `0.375 -> 0.068` Recall@20), whereas soft re-weighting preserves preference (`0.282`) while improving nutritional scores. Tastoria combines soft multi-objective tray scoring `(α, β, γ)` with hard safety constraints (`BR-ALG`, `BR-DIET`, `BR-TRAY-04/05`) and exact `OR-Tools CP-SAT` (`Google2025ortools` `E065`) cooking-step scheduling.

### 3.5 Statistical Inference & Reporting Standards
- **Paired Non-Parametric, Bootstrap & Multiplicity Control**: All cross-model comparisons follow `Efron1994introduction` (`E009`) paired/cluster bootstrap (`2,000+` resamples respecting cluster correlation), `Wilcoxon1945individual` (`E007`) paired signed-rank with rank-biserial & Hodges–Lehmann shift, and exact `McNemar1947note` (`E008`) / `Dietterich1998approximate` (`E010`) tests for paired binary outcomes. Confirmatory hypothesis families control family-wise error rate via **Holm–Bonferroni** (`Holm1979simple` `E005`), while exploratory subgroup/ablation families control false discovery rate via **Benjamini–Hochberg FDR** (`Benjamini1995controlling` `E006`).
- **AI Reporting Standards**: Study design, protocol preregistration, and final IMRAD reporting follow **TRIPOD+AI 2024** (`Collins2024tripodai` `E002`) for prediction/ranking/calibration models and **STARD 2015** (`Bossuyt2015stard` `E003`) + **STARD-AI 2025** (`Sounderajah2025stardai` `E004`) for diagnostic detection/freshness accuracy, orchestrated via K-Dense procedural skills (`Kassis2026skills` `E001`).
