## 4. "Mâm cơm Việt" Cultural Adaptation Rationale

Generic Western meal-planning UX patterns fail Vietnamese home cooks across four structural dimensions. Tastoria's design system turns these cultural realities into first-class visual and interaction primitives:

### 4.1 The 3-Dish + Rice Tray Ensemble (`Mặn` / `Rau` / `Canh` + `Cơm`)
- **Cultural Reality:** A Vietnamese family meal (*mâm cơm*) is not a single plated entrée; it is a shared, balanced ensemble centered on steamed rice (*cơm*) flanked by 1 savory protein dish (*Mặn*), 1 vegetable/greens dish (*Rau*), and 1 soup (*Canh*), calibrated for shared portions (*1–6 người ăn*, measured in *bát cơm*).
- **Design Adaptation:**
  - **Signature Motif:** Keep and elevate the top-down SVG **lacquered wooden tray (`Tray`)** with **Bát Tràng cobalt-rimmed porcelain dishes (`glaze` on `dish`)** in `domain.tsx` and the interactive `landing/` simulator.
  - **Responsive Spatial Mapping:** On Mobile (`<768px`), `Tray` sits prominently above the 3 slot cards (`Mặn`, `Rau`, `Canh`); on Desktop (`>=1024px`), `mam` opens into a **2-column split workspace** (Interactive `Tray` + `KcalBar` + Rice Bowl metric pinned on the left; Slot Swaps, Ranker rationale `reason_vi`, and Hybrid `lookup`/`embedding` Substitutes drawer on the right).

### 4.2 Perishable Market-Day Pantry Urgency (`tuoi` -> `dung_som` -> `dung_ngay` -> `kiem_tra`)
- **Cultural Reality:** Vietnamese kitchens buy small quantities of fresh herbs, leafy greens (*mớ rau*), tofu (*bìa đậu*), and meats daily at local markets (*đi chợ*), often without uniform barcode packaging or printed expiry dates.
- **Design Adaptation:**
  - **Urgency-First Hierarchy:** Pantry lots (`kho`) and Home strips (`bep`) sort by computed urgency, pairing **WCAG 1.4.1 dot-shape + color + Vietnamese text badges (`FreshBadge`)**: `tươi` (solid herb dot), `nên dùng sớm` (solid amber dot), `dùng ngay` (solid lacquer-danger dot), `kiểm tra trước khi dùng` / `chưa rõ độ tươi` (hollow ring dot — explicitly gated `available = false` until human confirmation).
  - **Human-in-the-Loop AI Vision (`quet/[id]`):** Calibrated Freshness ONNX probabilities (`p_tuoi`, `p_dung_som`, `p_dung_ngay`, `p_kiem_tra`) appear as transparent horizontal probability bars + bounding-box overlays so cooks understand *why* an item was flagged `kiem_tra` and can override count, `qty_level` (`ít` / `vừa` / `nhiều`), or freshness in 1 tap before writing to pantry.

### 4.3 Parallel Multi-Burner CP-SAT Cooking (`nau/[id]` + 10m Undo)
- **Cultural Reality:** Cooking *Mặn*, *Rau*, and *Canh* together for a fixed dinner time (`serve_at`) requires interleaving active wok/prep steps with passive simmering/steaming across 1–4 gas/induction burners plus a rice cooker.
- **Design Adaptation:**
  - **CP-SAT Gantt Timeline:** Visualize each dish track with high-contrast **solid terracotta `active` bars** (hands-on cooking) vs **calm herb-green `passive` bars** (simmering/resting) plus a live `"bây giờ"` vertical marker.
  - **Kitchen-Safe Ergonomics:** Oversized `28px` active-step card, `>=48dp` (`TOUCH`) buttons readable at arm's length with damp hands, wake-lock (`useScreenOn`), **absolute-timestamp multi-timers** (`endAt - now` so phone sleep never causes drift), offline-resilient completion outbox, and a prominent **10-minute Undo countdown** (`Hoàn tác (còn mm:ss)`) in `nau-xong/[id]`.

### 4.4 Vietnamese Diacritic Legibility & Numeric Precision (`Be Vietnam Pro`)
- **Linguistic Reality:** Vietnamese stacks tone and vowel diacritics vertically (e.g., *Bữa cơm*, *Đậu phụ*, *Cần kiểm tra*, *Ở*, *Giữ lại*, *Nghệ*). Tight line-heights (`< 1.4`), aggressive negative letter-spacing on small text, or unvetted Latin display fonts clip upper/lower diacritics or blur *hỏi / ngã / nặng* marks.
- **Design Adaptation:**
  - **Canonical Typeface:** `Be Vietnam Pro` (`400`, `500`, `600`, `700`) is mandated across both `landing/` and `app/` because its glyph metrics provide generous ascender/descender clearance for stacked Vietnamese marks.
  - **Strict Tracking & Height Floor:** Tight negative tracking (`-0.06em` to `-0.085em`) is permitted **only** on large display headlines (`>= 36px`); body (`15–16px`) and labels (`12–14px`) MUST use `letter-spacing >= 0` and `line-height >= 1.5` (`1.5–1.7`), with `tabular-nums` enforced on every calorie readout, gram weight, countdown timer, and admin/ML metric.
